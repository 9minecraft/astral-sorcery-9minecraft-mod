# Astral Sorcery 9minecraft Mod - Channel Starlight Into Powerful Magic

Astral Sorcery 9minecraft Mod lets you pull magic from the night sky. You can study the stars to find hidden constellations and use their light to craft powerful items. The mod adds special altars that collect starlight to power up your gear and give you extra abilities. You also get a helpful guide book to track your progress as you build grand temples and observatories. Features: The Astral Tome: A handy guide book that tracks every constellation you find and shows you all the crafting recipes.
